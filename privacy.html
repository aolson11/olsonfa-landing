Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Olson Franchise Advisory ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website olsonfa.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit the contact form on our website (name, email address, phone number)
  • Complete the Franchise Fit Assessment (professional background, investment preferences, timeline)
  • Communicate with us by email or phone

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the purpose of:

  • Evaluating your franchise ownership suitability and providing personalized recommendations
  • Contacting you in response to your inquiries
  • Sending you relevant franchise insights and updates (only if you explicitly opt in)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We use FormSubmit.co as a service provider to transmit form submissions to an owner-controlled work inbox (admin@olsoncg.com). Olson Franchise Advisory receives and can access those submissions for the purposes described above, and FormSubmit.co processes the submission according to its own terms and privacy practices.

Data Security

We implement appropriate measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at austin@olsonfa.com.

Cookies

Our website does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated revision date.
